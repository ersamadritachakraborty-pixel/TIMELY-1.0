## Overview

**Timely** is a thoughtfully engineered reminder and notification platform developed as part of the professional internship programme at the **National Institute of Electronics & Information Technology (NIELIT), Agartala**.  

The system is built upon a singular, non-negotiable principle: **reminders must arrive at the exact moment they are needed** — neither prematurely nor after the opportunity has passed. By prioritising temporal precision and contextual relevance, Timely transforms ordinary notifications into actionable intelligence.

---

## Core Philosophy

> *“Getting reminders right before it’s too late.”*

In an age of information overload, the value of a reminder is determined not by its volume but by its **timing**. Timely has been designed to minimise cognitive friction and maximise response effectiveness through carefully calibrated delivery windows.

---

## Key Features

- **Precision Timing Engine** — Configurable lead-time and absolute-time triggers with intelligent buffering  
- **Context-Aware Delivery** — Adaptive notification strategy based on user activity patterns  
- **Multi-Channel Support** — Seamless integration across web, mobile, and desktop environments  
- **Reliable Persistence** — Robust scheduling with failure recovery and retry mechanisms  
- **Clean, Minimal Interface** — Distraction-free design focused on clarity and actionability  
- **Audit & History** — Complete traceability of reminder lifecycle for accountability and review
